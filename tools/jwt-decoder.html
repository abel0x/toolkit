JWT Decoder

Decode JSON Web Tokens and verify their signatures — header, payload, expiry and claims.

Decoding does not verify the signature. Check it below with the secret or public key, and never trust a JWT without server-side verification.

Verify the signature

The algorithm comes from the token's header. HS256, HS384, HS512: the shared secret. RS, PS and ES (256, 384, 512) and EdDSA: the public key as a PEM (BEGIN PUBLIC KEY), a JWK or a JWKS, where the header's kid picks the key. Nothing leaves this browser.
